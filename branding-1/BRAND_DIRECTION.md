# NovaIM — Brand Direction 2026

An online brand proposal, presented as a minimal scrolling deck.
Positioning: NovaIM builds the intelligence infrastructure behind modern companies.
Brand idea: Intelligence, built in.
Creative concept: Invisible intelligence. Visible structure.

## Accepted visual direction
- The first screen is ONLY the centered NovaIM logo on black.
- Circular dot symbol reconstructed from the reference: seven staggered rows with 2/3/4/3/4/3/2 dots, increasing in size toward the bottom. Dots gather into the mark and hold before repeating.
- Zalando Sans throughout, letter spacing -0.04em. No Geist.
- Carbon #080A09, white #FFFFFF, mint #62D8AA, forest #173D32, lilac #C4A1E8, plum #34283F. No blue accent.
- Less text, fewer small labels, larger conceptual cards.
- Flat point animations must communicate meaning: independent events pulse (signals), dispersed points align (systems), packets move along persistent lanes (infrastructure).
- No decorative 3D particle volumes.
- NovaIM is calm and structural; HIVE is expressive and operational.

## Narrative
Logo → Direction / Shift → Visual concept cards → Principles → Identity → Applications / HIVE → Closing.
English copy. Responsive layout. Global motion pause and reduced-motion support.
Wordmark reconstruction and applications remain brand proposal explorations.

## Implementation
Next.js App Router, server-rendered page, isolated client canvas components, CSS logo animation. Read installed Next.js docs before framework changes.

## Refinement
Use predominantly black and pure white backgrounds, with mint/lilac accents. Concept cards are animation-first, unnumbered, followed by title and description. Concepts: clarity from complexity, intelligence in concert, a foundation for flow. Pointer movement changes focus, gathers signals, and selects routes; click/tap/keyboard triggers a signal. Respect reduced motion and global pause.

### Visual idea refinement
Restore the original dark green, forest green and plum concept-card backgrounds. “Visible structure” is gray. No visible hover instructions. Clarity and Flow have continuously traveling border signals that reverse and split on hover/focus. Flow also autonomously selects lanes; hover selects a crossing and accelerates its traffic. Global pause and reduced-motion preferences apply to the border animation too.

Latest refinement: remove traveling card borders and divider lines. Concept graphics use a square area and square 29×29 grid with equal margins. Cards are shorter, with subtle background transitions on hover/focus. The Visual Idea label is brighter and closer to its heading.

Positioning section: label “Brand positioning”, headline “The intelligence infrastructure behind modern companies.” Supporting copy “One connected foundation. An entire ecosystem of possibilities.” Right-side monochrome interactive particle composition inspired by the supplied cloud/ring reference: dispersed signals flow into a stable circular foundation. This reference-driven composition is an exception to the earlier flat-only direction.

Additional refinements: Visual concept label without numbering; omit the summary under those cards. Principles use a light gray background, black text, and animated point studies. Applications use only the symbol, matching green/plum backgrounds, gray secondary heading, and distinct momentum/radiating-interference animations.

Intro now uses native scroll storytelling: a large white point → a signal caption → the point shrinks and surrounding dots assemble the exact circular mark → Zalando Sans NovaIM wordmark appears → final complete logo. Scroll is reversible, with a skip link and a static full-story reduced-motion layout.

Positioning refinement: remove the navigation bar entirely and the positioning number. Brighter Brand positioning label. Replace cloud/ring composition with one precisely spaced circular annulus: stable concentric dot lanes with traveling signal intensity and pointer response, no silhouette deformation.
