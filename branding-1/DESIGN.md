# Novaim design guide

The [Brand Direction 2026 guide](https://novaim-branding.vercel.app/branding1/) uses green and neutral colors.

## Palette

| Color | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon | `#080A09` | Dark backgrounds and text on light surfaces. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Light surfaces and text on dark surfaces. |
| Mint | `#62D8AA` | Accent, signals, selection and emphasis. |
| Forest | `#173D32` | Deep green surfaces and brand ink. |
| Gray | `#929A95` | Secondary text on dark backgrounds and neutral surfaces. |

Supporting shades stay green or neutral. Animation interpolation, hover states, palette examples and raster mockups follow these same colors. Keep text contrast at least 4.5:1 and do not use color as the only state indicator.

## Identity and behavior

Preserve the official monochrome symbol and wordmark geometry. Use Zalando Sans with the existing regular typography. Preserve the guide's layout, native scrolling, motion pause and reduced-motion behavior.
